The problem

Battery thermal runaway is now a public-safety crisis

As electric-vehicle and storage deployments scale, fire incidents are rising faster than the fleet itself — and regulators are responding with the strictest safety standard the industry has ever seen.

⏱️

< 5 minutes to escape

From the first sign of fire to a fully engulfed vehicle, occupants have less than five minutes — enough to escape, not enough to intervene.

📈

+40% incidents / year

Battery-fire accidents are growing ~40% annually — roughly 10 percentage points faster than the battery fleet itself.

💸

> $50B lost in 2024

Global economic losses from battery fires and related incidents exceeded fifty billion US dollars in 2024 alone.

Regulation is shifting from "buy time" to "absolute safety"

GB 38031-2020 · current

≥ 5 minutes with no fire or explosion after thermal runaway, plus a warning signal.

GB 38031-2025 · upcoming

≥ 2 hours with no fire or explosion — an "absolute safety" bar that demands a fundamentally new approach.

Why today's solutions fall short

Four separate systems, four compromises

Validated through in-depth interviews with 70+ manufacturers and regulators across China, Europe and Southeast Asia, today's packs bolt together independent subsystems that each solve only part of the problem.

Keeps cells warm in cold conditions to preserve performance.

✗ High energy use; needs its own system

Cooling

✗ Bulky and structurally complex

Slows heat conduction between cells.

✗ Limited effect; can't stop explosion
🧯

Extinguishes a fire once it has started.

✗ Reacts late; can feed combustion

✗ Compromise

✗ Non-compliant

No existing approach meets the integrated GB 38031-2025 bar.

How it works

Phase-change regulation + local fire confinement

A single phase-change mechanism delivers two-way thermal control across a wide operating window of −40 °C to +200 °C.

↓

Absorb & cool

At high temperature the material absorbs large amounts of heat as it changes phase, blocking heat build-up and capping cell temperature before runaway can start.

↑

Release & warm

At low temperature it releases stored heat, keeping cells within their ideal operating range — no external heater required.

Confine the fire

Limits a fire to the originating cell, protecting neighbouring cells.

Stop propagation

Halts the chain reaction so the whole pack stays safe.

No failure modes

No electronics, no mechanics — nothing to break or maintain.

Experimental validation

1.5 mm stops runaway completely

Two cells were stacked with a single 1.5 mm Battery Shield layer between them. The lower cell was overcharged to trigger thermal runaway while both cell temperatures were monitored.

> 500 °C

Peak temperature of the overcharged source cell

< 65 °C

Peak temperature of the protected cell behind the shield

Result: no fire propagation, no explosion, and almost no measurable degradation of the protected cell — meeting the GB 38031-2025 requirement. Test methodology and results were reviewed and recognised by leading battery manufacturers interviewed during validation.
